Who We Are

A foundation built by volunteers, for the gaps no one else was filling.

InAmigos started as a small group of students who kept noticing the same unmet needs — a child without a classroom, a family without a meal, an animal without anyone to call. Rather than picking one cause, we built six, each run by the volunteers and interns closest to the problem.

Every program stands on its own, with its own team and its own measure of success. Together, they're how InAmigos shows up across 28 states — not as a single charity, but as a working network of people who decided to stop waiting for someone else to start.

Our Programs

Six causes, one direction.

Each program is a complete answer to a need we kept seeing repeat — run independently, measured honestly, and open to anyone willing to show up.

01 Relief & Essentials

Seva

Food, clothing, and everyday essentials, delivered straight into the hands of families who need them most. Seva is where InAmigos shows up first — and stays.

02 Education & Care

Bachpanshala

A classroom and a caregiver for children whose schooling would otherwise wait. Bachpanshala turns spare hours into steady, dependable education.

03 Animal Welfare

Jeev

Rescue, treatment, and shelter for animals with no one else advocating for them. Jeev exists to give the voiceless a second chance.

04 Women Empowerment

Udaan

Skill-building and real opportunity, designed to help women move from dependence to direction. Udaan is named for flight — and it shows.

05 Environment

Prakriti

Clean-up drives and plantation work that give the land room to recover. Prakriti is the long game — planted today, felt for decades.

06 Youth & Internship

Vikas

Internships and mentorship that turn ambition into real skill. Vikas is how today's interns become tomorrow's changemakers.

Over 250 interns have chosen to stay — not as volunteers, but as permanent members of this family.
